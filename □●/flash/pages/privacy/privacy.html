Privacy notice

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This web app connects to your Squares & Circles module over USB in your browser, shows its firmware and apps and updates them. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics service. The data of your module stay in your browser, except those of the module register (section 3).

2. Hosting and content from GitHub

The page is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA); your browser also loads the firmware list, the apps, the loaders and the release notes from GitHub. GitHub receives your IP address and the usual data of a web request and logs them. A copy for trying out new versions, squares-and-circles.eh2k.workers.dev, is served by Cloudflare (section 3), which then receives these data instead. Other sites (ROM downloads, Teensy Loader) are only contacted when you click their links. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in providing the page reliably. GitHub and Cloudflare are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

3. Module register

When a module connects, the page sends its serial number, type and firmware version to our server (squares-and-circles.eh2k.workers.dev), once per module, firmware version and browser tab. The server keeps per module: serial number, type, firmware version and since when, first and last report and how many, whether it is a test module that gets the next release before it is published, and a note of ours. It answers whether the module gets that release. It also counts, without the serial number, how many modules are new, updated to another firmware version or active per month, by type and firmware version; these counts are anonymous and kept. It does not keep your IP address, where you are, your browser's details or the single reports; to find errors, Cloudflare logs each request for us (your IP address and browser among its data) for at most 7 days. There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

Purpose: to know how many modules use the firmware and which versions, to help with problems and to give test modules the release under test. An entry is deleted 24 months after its last report (a test module's when it is no longer one), on request at once. Processor: Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, under its Data Processing Addendum. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in these purposes; the page reads these data from your module anyway, for the update you asked for (§ 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG). The report is not required by law or contract. You can object to it by an e-mail to the address in section 1 (Art. 21 GDPR); we then delete the entry and keep only the serial number, marked so that its reports are no longer stored.

4. Stored in your browser

The type of module you chose last, the serial number of the module connected last (to find it again) and that you have seen the note on the module register (local storage), and for the browser tab the modules already reported with the server's answers (session storage). You can delete them with your browser's site data. Legal basis: § 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–18, 20, 21 GDPR): write an e-mail to the address in section 1. You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU member state where you live or work.
