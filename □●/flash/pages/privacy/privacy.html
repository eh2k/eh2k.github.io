Privacy notice

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This web app connects to your Squares & Circles module over USB in your browser, shows its firmware and apps and updates them. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics service. The data of your module stay in your browser, except those of the module register (section 3) and, if you connect an AI assistant, those it asks for (section 4).

2. Hosting and content from GitHub

The page is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA); your browser also loads the firmware list, the apps, the loaders and the release notes from GitHub. GitHub receives your IP address and the usual data of a web request and logs them. A copy for trying out new versions, squares-and-circles.eh2k.workers.dev, is served by Cloudflare (section 3), which then receives these data instead. The compiler and the apps' libraries the page loads when an app is first built in your browser (about 35 MB, files of our GitHub repository) come through the same Cloudflare service on both pages: Cloudflare receives that request, GitHub only Cloudflare's. A link you share from this page (a patch, a calibration, an app's source code) carries that content in its address, which the host receives when the link is opened. Other sites (ROM downloads, Teensy Loader) are only contacted when you click their links. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in providing the page reliably. GitHub and Cloudflare are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

3. Module register

Each time a module connects, the page sends its serial number, type and firmware version to our server (squares-and-circles.eh2k.workers.dev). The server keeps per module: serial number, type, firmware version and since when, first and last report and how many, whether it is an Insiders module that gets the next release before it is published (and whether also our developer pages), and a note of ours. It answers whether the module gets that release (and those pages). It also counts, without the serial number, how many modules are new, updated to another firmware version or active per month, by type and firmware version; these counts are anonymous and kept. It does not keep your IP address, where you are, your browser's details or the single reports; to find errors, Cloudflare logs each request for us (your IP address and browser among its data) for at most 7 days. There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

Purpose: to know how many modules use the firmware and which versions, to help with problems and to give Insiders modules the release under test. An entry is deleted 24 months after its last report (an Insiders module's when it is no longer one), on request at once. Processor: Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, under its Data Processing Addendum. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in these purposes; the page reads these data from your module anyway, for the update you asked for (§ 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG). The report is not required by law or contract. You can object to it by an e-mail to the address in section 1 (Art. 21 GDPR); we then delete the entry and keep only the serial number, marked so that its reports are no longer stored.

4. AI assistant (optional)

While Let's vibe is open (and its MCP on, which it is when it opens), the page connects your own AI assistant (an MCP client such as Claude or Copilot) to the page's emulator. While it is connected, the page holds a connection to our server (squares-and-circles.eh2k.workers.dev), and the assistant's requests and the page's answers pass through it: the apps it plays in the emulator (also as source code, which your browser compiles itself) and what the emulator shows and plays (its display, levels, parameters). Your module is not part of it: [Run on module] and [Install on module] put the app shown onto your module only when you click them, directly over USB. The server does not keep these data (Cloudflare logs the requests as in section 3). It keeps, without the address or any content, per day how often each of the assistant's tools was called and whether it worked, and the notes an assistant sends when the tools could not answer or do what you asked (in the assistant's own words, at most 500 characters); we use them to see what the tools are used for and what is missing, and delete them after 12 months. The address stays the same in this browser (derived from a random key the page keeps in your browser, section 5), it works only while the page is connected, and whoever has it can reach the emulator of your page then. What your assistant does with the data is up to its provider and your agreement with them. Processor: Cloudflare (section 3). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, you ask for it; for the counts and notes Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in improving the tools.

5. Stored in your browser

The type of module you chose last, the serial number of the module connected last (to find it again), that you have seen the note on the module register and, if you use the AI assistant, the random key of its address and the app it played last in Let's vibe (local storage), and the compiler the page loads when an app is built here for the first time (section 2; cache storage). You can delete them with your browser's site data. Legal basis: § 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG.
