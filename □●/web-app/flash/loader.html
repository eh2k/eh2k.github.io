Flashing Squares&Circles

For a bare Teensy, the S&C loader firmware must be installed first. This can be done directly in your browser (tested with Chrome) following this steps:

Stage 1: Flashing Squares&Circles Loader
  1. Connect the Teensy4 to your PC using a USB cable.
  2. Click [Reset] - connect to USB device listed in the browser-popup.
    • If no USB device is listet, Teensy is likely in the flashing mode, follow step 3
  3. Click [Flash] - connect to 'HID 16C0:0478' device listed in the browser-popup.
    • Ensure the TeensyLoader is not running in background.
    • If no USB device is listet, press the Button on the teensy, and click [Flash].

If your broswer is not supporting the serial interface, you can download the Squares&Circles loader hex file and flash it with the Teensy Loader.
After flashing the loader the device startups only showing the splash screen with a blinking "Ready to Update".
